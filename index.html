🔬 The Factor Analysis Framework

Dimensionality Reduction

Precision agricultural IoT networks deploy dozens of correlated physical sensors. High multicollinearity complicates direct modeling and decision-making. Factor Analysis decomposes \(p=11\) observed sensor measurements into \(k=3\) fundamental, uncorrelated latent dimensions:

x_i - \mu_i = \lambda_{i1} F_{\text{Fertility}} + \lambda_{i2} F_{\text{Moisture}} + \lambda_{i3} F_{\text{Vigor}} + \epsilon_i

Where \(\lambda_{ij}\) represents the factor loading, \(F_j\) represents the unobservable latent state, and \(\epsilon_i\) is unique sensor variance.
